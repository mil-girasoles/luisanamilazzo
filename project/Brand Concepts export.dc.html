Two directions for one voice

Both ideas start from the same position: about 20 years bridging business and IT, now applied to AI. They share the Industry system (steel, Barlow, blueprint frames) and differ in the central metaphor. 1a builds the brand from engineering. 1b builds it from route-finding in the mountains.

1a Concept · Engineering

The Span

A bridge is a structure that carries load between two sides. Luisana's career has done the same between business and IT, and the same span now reaches AI. The brand is drawn like a technical drawing: measured, load-tested, annotated.

1b Concept · Cartography

The Route

In the mountains you read the terrain, set waypoints and only then start walking. Luisana works the same way: analysis, roadmap, prototype, go-live. Her own route runs from Caracas to Hannover, and the brand uses the language of maps to show it.

Signature motif · waypoints and elevation

Content is laid out as numbered waypoints that climb toward a summit marker. It works for a delivery method, a career timeline (Caracas 2000 → INSEAD 2026) or an agenda slide.

Tagline
A clear route through transformation.
Alt: "Read the terrain. Then move."
Voice
Oriented. Say where we are, where we're going, and the next step.
Experienced. Speak like a guide who has walked the route before.
Calm. Steady pace, no urgency language.
Sample line
"Before any AI tool, I map the terrain: processes, data, people. Then we set waypoints everyone can see."
